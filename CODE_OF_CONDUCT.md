# Code Of Conduct

DREAM ONLINE contributors should keep collaboration direct, respectful, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be clear and specific.
- Discuss systems, code, and design tradeoffs without personal attacks.
- Respect project boundaries around secrets, classified material, and ownership.
- Keep feedback actionable.
- Protect players from exploitative, unsafe, or confusing systems.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or abuse.
- Publishing secrets or private material.
- Copying competitor content or brand assets.
- Adding hidden control paths, backdoors, or unauthorized telemetry.
- Intentionally weakening safety, security, or player trust.

## Enforcement

The project owner may remove content, revoke access, or close contributions that violate
these rules.
