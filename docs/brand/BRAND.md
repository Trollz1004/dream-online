# DREAM ONLINE Brand System

Status: draft

## Position

DREAM ONLINE is a live-world open-world MMO built around action combat, life skills,
player-driven markets, high-stakes PvP, and live NPCs that make the world feel awake.

## Voice

- Clear, readable, and player-first.
- Big fantasy without developer slang.
- Serious when describing risk.
- Warm when guiding new players.
- Never vendor-branded inside the world.

## Avoid

- Direct competitor name drops.
- Real-world brand names for in-game systems.
- `Sandbox` jargon in player-facing copy.
- Private accounting, split, charity, or legal-structure language.
- Pay-to-win promises or paid power framing.

## Visual direction

- Moon gate.
- Gold horizon.
- Deep blue wilderness.
- City-light warmth.
- Dream event glow.
- Dark Nightfall contrast.
