# Gemini HUD Terms Check (Google's Rules, Read by Gemini) — 2026-11-10

**Overall Verdict:** Google Gemini, Google Chrome, Google Search, and Gmail are products and trademarks of Google LLC, not DREAM Online or our LLC, and under Google's current terms (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms`, effective March 23, 2026, read 2026-11-10; `https://developers.google.com/terms`, read 2026-11-10) we cannot offer a Gemini API-based HUD to every player in an all-ages game, cannot name our own in-game features or NPCs `Gemini HUD`, `GeminEYE`, or `Gemini Jewls`, cannot feed Gmail inbox data into the unpaid Gemini API, and cannot give each player their own separate cloud Gemini API free-tier quota through a single app OAuth Client ID. To stay at $0 cost and 100% compliant with Google's terms today, the in-game goggles must use an original house name, state plainly that the assistant is Google LLC's product, use Chrome's local Built-in AI (`Prompt API` / Gemini Nano) and Chrome's `Web Speech API` (or open Google's own signed-in web tabs for Gemini, Google Search, and Gmail compose), and restrict any direct Gemini API connection to verified 18+ adults outside the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK.

## 1. Age Limit: Can a Gemini API-based HUD be offered to every player, or only behind an 18+ gate?

**Verdict:** **Confirmed, and stricter than an in-game toggle: a Gemini API-based HUD cannot be offered to every player at all, and under the literal text of the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service, the Gemini API cannot be embedded in an all-ages game likely to be accessed by minors unless the API client is strictly isolated behind a verified 18+ gate and blocked in the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK on the unpaid tier.**

- **What Google's primary terms say:** The **Age Requirements** section of the **Gemini API Additional Terms of Service** (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms` and `https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms`, effective March 23, 2026, read 2026-11-10) states word for word: *"You must be 18 years of age or older to use the APIs. You also will not use the Services as part of a website, application, or other service (collectively, 'API Clients') that is directed towards or is likely to be accessed by individuals under the age of 18."*
- **Why every player is barred:** Offering a Gemini API-based HUD to all players in a game that is free to all and welcoming to minors directly violates the clause barring use in any service likely to be accessed by individuals under 18 (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms`, read 2026-11-10).
- **Why an 18+ gate must isolate the API client:** The terms define the entire website, application, or service as the "API Client" (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms`, read 2026-11-10). Even with an in-game 18+ toggle, putting a Gemini API client inside an all-ages game that minors are likely to access risks violating the terms unless the Gemini API integration lives in a separate, adult-gated companion module that minors cannot reach and that is never marketed to players under 18.
- **Additional EEA, Switzerland, and UK bar on the free tier:** The same **Gemini API Additional Terms of Service** (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms`, read 2026-11-10) state: *"You may use only Paid Services when making API Clients available to users in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom."* Because nothing may cost money until Josh says finalize, a free-tier Gemini API client cannot be made available to players in the EEA, Switzerland, or the UK at all.
- **What is allowed for all players at $0:** Standard browser speech-to-text through Chrome's W3C **Web Speech API** (`https://developer.chrome.com/blog/voice-driven-web-apps-introduction-to-the-web-speech-api`, read 2026-11-10), searching our own DREAM Online recipe forum directly, and opening ordinary browser links to Google Search or Gmail in the player's own Chrome browser do not call the Gemini Developer API and are not restricted by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service.

## 2. Brand Features and Naming: May an in-game NPC or feature be named `Gemini`, `Gemini Jewls`, `Gemini HUD`, or `GeminEYE`?

**Verdict:** **Confirmed: we may not name our own in-game features, items, pets, or NPCs `Gemini HUD`, `GeminEYE`, or `Gemini Jewls`. We must give our in-game goggles, pet, and jeweler NPC original house names and use the word `Gemini™` only in plain descriptive sentences stating that the HUD connects to Google LLC's product.**

- **What Google's API Terms say:** **Section 6 (Brand Features; Attribution)** of the **Google APIs Terms of Service** (`https://developers.google.com/terms`, read 2026-11-10) states in **Section 6a** that the terms grant no right, title, or interest in Google's Brand Features, and states in **Section 6b** that Google grants a limited license to display Google's Brand Features strictly *"for the purpose of promoting or advertising that you use the APIs"* in accordance with the **Google Brand Features Use Guidelines**. **Section 6b (Publicity)** states: *"You will not make any statement regarding your use of an API which suggests partnership with, sponsorship by, or endorsement by Google without Google's prior written approval."*
- **What Google's Brand Resource Center rules say:** Google's **Brand Resource Center Rules** (`https://about.google/brand-resource-center/rules/` and `https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/trademark/`, read 2026-11-10) state:
  - *"Don't incorporate Google Brand Features into your own product name, service name, trademark, logo, or company name."*
  - *"Don't remove, distort, or alter any element of a Google Brand Feature. That includes modifying a Google trademark, for example, through hyphenation, combination, or abbreviation."*
- **Ruling on `Gemini HUD`:** Not allowed as the name of our in-game item or interface. Naming our goggles "the Gemini HUD" incorporates Google's `Gemini™` trademark (`https://about.google/brand-resource-center/trademark-list/`, read 2026-11-10) into our own product feature name. Instead, the item must carry an original house name (for example, `Optic HUD` or `Sight Goggles`) with a plain description: *"Connects to Google Gemini™ (a product of Google LLC) using your own Google Account."*
- **Ruling on `GeminEYE`:** Not allowed. `GeminEYE` combines and alters the `Gemini™` trademark (`Gemini` + `EYE`), which directly violates Google's rule against modifying a trademark through combination or punning (`https://about.google/brand-resource-center/rules/`, read 2026-11-10) as well as our house originality rule in `docs/house/THE-HOUSE.md`.
- **Ruling on `Gemini Jewls`:** Do not use. Although "Gemini" is also the Latin name of the zodiac twins, naming a commercial shop NPC `Gemini Jewls` inside a game that integrates with Google Gemini and credits Google Gemini on its surfaces creates trademark confusion and implies Google branding under `https://about.google/brand-resource-center/rules/` (read 2026-11-10) and `https://developers.google.com/terms` Section 6 (read 2026-11-10). Use an original English or in-house name (such as `Twin Jewels` or an original character name) so `Gemini™` appears solely when referring truthfully to Google LLC's assistant.

## 3. Official Browser Path and Cost: Which official path lets a player use their own Google sign-in and free tier inside a browser game, and what does each path cost us?

**Verdict:** **Sign in with Google plus Gemini API user OAuth costs us $0 if we never attach a billing account, but it does NOT give each player their own separate Gemini API quota—every OAuth request made through our app's OAuth Client ID draws from our single Google Cloud project's free-tier rate limits, and Google Search grounding is blocked on the free tier. For each player to use their own free quota at $0 cost to us, the game must use Chrome's local Built-in AI (`Prompt API` / Gemini Nano) plus Chrome's `Web Speech API` and direct links to Google's signed-in web apps, or let an 18+ player supply their own personal AI Studio API key.**

- **Path A — Sign in with Google + Gemini API with User OAuth (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/oauth`, read 2026-11-10):**
  - **How it works:** The browser uses the **Google Identity Services SDK** (`https://developers.google.com/identity/gsi/web/guides/overview`, read 2026-11-10) to sign the player in and request OAuth scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/generative-language` (or `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/generative-language.retriever`), calling `https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com` directly from the browser.
  - **Cost to us:** **$0.00** as long as our Google Cloud project stays on the Free Tier with no Cloud Billing account linked (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/billing`, read 2026-11-10). Sign in with Google itself is always $0 (`https://developers.google.com/identity/gsi/web/guides/overview`, read 2026-11-10).
  - **Quota bottleneck:** According to **Gemini API Rate Limits** (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits`, read 2026-11-10), rate limits (Requests Per Minute, Tokens Per Minute, Requests Per Day) are enforced **per Google Cloud project, not per user**. Every player authenticating through our OAuth Client ID shares our single project's free-tier quota pool and will hit `429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` once that single free pool is full.
  - **Web Search cost block:** According to **Grounding with Google Search** and **Gemini API Pricing** (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/google-search` and `https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing`, read 2026-11-10), Grounding with Google Search is **not available on the Free Tier**; it requires a Paid Tier billing account (5,000 free search requests per month on the Paid Tier, then $14 per 1,000 search queries). Furthermore, Google's **Custom Search JSON API** (`https://developers.google.com/custom-search/v1/overview`, read 2026-11-10) is closed to new customers, sunsets on January 1, 2027, and only gave 100 free queries per day per project ($5 per 1,000 thereafter).
- **Path B — Chrome Built-in AI (`Prompt API` / Gemini Nano) + Chrome `Web Speech API` + Signed-In Google Web Surfaces (`https://developer.chrome.com/docs/ai/built-in-apis` and `https://developer.chrome.com/docs/ai/prompt-api`, read 2026-11-10):**
  - **How it works:** Inside Google Chrome, the game uses Chrome's built-in **Web Speech API** (`https://developer.chrome.com/blog/voice-driven-web-apps-introduction-to-the-web-speech-api`, read 2026-11-10) for talk-to-text, Chrome's **Built-in AI Prompt API** (`LanguageModel`, powered locally by Google's **Gemini Nano** inside Chrome) for local assistant parsing, our own forum API for recipe search, and standard browser URLs (`https://www.google.com/search?q=...`, `https://gemini.google.com/`, and `https://mail.google.com/mail/?view=cm&fs=1&...`) where the player is already signed in with their personal Google Account.
  - **Cost to us:** **$0.00 forever.** All speech and Gemini Nano inference runs on the player's own hardware inside Chrome, with zero cloud API bills and zero shared project rate limits.
- **Path C — Player's Own Google AI Studio Free-Tier Key in Browser Memory (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/api-key` and `https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits`, read 2026-11-10):**
  - **How it works:** An 18+ player outside the EEA/UK creates a personal free-tier API key in their own Google AI Studio account (`https://aistudio.google.com/apikey`) and pastes it into browser memory (`sessionStorage`, never sent to our servers).
  - **Cost to us:** **$0.00**, and requests count against that player's own Google Cloud project free tier (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits`, read 2026-11-10). However, Grounding with Google Search is still unavailable on the Free Tier (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing`, read 2026-11-10), and the 18+ age rule still applies (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms`, read 2026-11-10).

## 4. Gmail Scopes: Which scopes are Restricted or Sensitive, and do they need Google verification or a paid security assessment?

**Verdict:** **Every Gmail scope that reads, searches, or drafts inbox mail is Restricted (`gmail.readonly`, `gmail.modify`, `gmail.compose`, `gmail.metadata`, `mail.google.com`), requires Google Restricted Scope Verification plus an annual paid third-party CASA security assessment if mail data or tokens touch our servers, and is barred by Google's Workspace policy from being sent to the free-tier Gemini API. Sending mail only (`gmail.send`) is Sensitive (free Google verification, no CASA audit), and opening a pre-filled Gmail compose tab in Chrome needs zero Gmail API scopes.**

- **Restricted Gmail scopes (reading, searching, composing drafts, or full access):** According to **Choose Gmail API scopes** (`https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes`, read 2026-11-10), Google classifies all of the following scopes as **Restricted**:
  - `https://mail.google.com/` (Full access to the account's mailboxes)
  - `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly` (Read all resources and their metadata — required to read or search a player's emails)
  - `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify` (All read/write operations except immediate, permanent deletion)
  - `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose` (Create, read, update, and delete drafts; send messages and drafts)
  - `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.insert` (Insert and import messages)
  - `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.metadata` (Read resources metadata including labels, history, and headers, but not message body)
  - `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.basic` and `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.sharing` (Manage mail settings)
- **Sensitive Gmail scopes (sending only):** According to `https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes` (read 2026-11-10), `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send` (Send messages only; no read access) is classified as **Sensitive**, not Restricted.
- **Verification and CASA security assessment rules:** According to **Restricted Scope Verification** (`https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/restricted-scope-verification`, read 2026-11-10) and the **Google API Services User Data Policy** (`https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy`, read 2026-11-10):
  1. **Sensitive Scope Verification (`gmail.send`):** Requires Google OAuth App Verification (domain ownership, privacy policy, and a YouTube demonstration video showing the consent flow). Google charges **$0** for Sensitive Scope Verification, and no third-party security assessment is required. Until verified, the OAuth client stays in Testing mode and is capped at 100 test users.
  2. **Restricted Scope Verification (`gmail.readonly`, `gmail.modify`, `gmail.compose`):** Restricted scopes are only approved for specific permitted application categories (such as dedicated email clients or backup tools; an in-game HUD is unlikely to be approved to read a user's inbox). Furthermore, if restricted Gmail data or tokens ever pass through or are stored on a server, Google mandates an annual third-party **Cloud Application Security Assessment (CASA)** conducted by an authorized lab (`https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/production-readiness/restricted-scope-verification`, read 2026-11-10), which costs money and violates our $0 rule until Josh says finalize.
  3. **Conflict between Restricted Gmail scopes and the Free-Tier Gemini API:** The **Google Workspace APIs User Data and Developer Policy** (`https://developers.google.com/workspace/workspace-api-user-data-developer-policy`, read 2026-11-10) and **Google API Services User Data Policy** (`https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy`, read 2026-11-10) prohibit transferring Workspace user data (including Gmail content) to train or improve generalized, non-personalized AI/ML models or allowing humans to read it. Because the Unpaid (Free Tier) Gemini API uses submitted prompts to train Google's generalized ML models and allows human review (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms`, read 2026-11-10), sending a player's Gmail inbox contents into the Free-Tier Gemini API is forbidden.
  4. **Compliant $0 solution for "send mail" in `GAME-SYSTEMS-RULING-2026-11-07.md`:** Canon line 20 of `docs/gdd/GAME-SYSTEMS-RULING-2026-11-07.md` specifies *"to browse, send mail, and search our forum for the recipe of a food or elixir"* (not reading inbox mail). If the HUD transcribes the player's dictated message via Chrome's `Web Speech API` and opens a pre-filled Gmail compose tab (`https://mail.google.com/mail/?view=cm&fs=1&to=...&su=...&body=...`) so the player clicks Send in their own browser—or uses `gmail.send` directly from the browser without routing mail through the free-tier Gemini API—it requires zero Restricted scopes and zero CASA audit fees.

## 5. Attribution, Wording, and Logo Rules: Is there an approved way to say "powered by Google Gemini" in the game?

**Verdict:** **Yes, plain-text factual attribution is allowed, provided our game's own name is always larger and more prominent, we never imply Google partnership or endorsement, we include trademark symbols and a clear disclaimer that Google Gemini™ is a product of Google LLC, and we follow strict Sign in with Google button rules without placing standalone Google or Gemini spark logos inside the game UI.**

- **Exact wording rules (`https://developers.google.com/terms` Section 6 and `https://about.google/brand-resource-center/rules/`, read 2026-11-10):**
  - You may make truthful, plain-text statements describing that a feature connects to or is built with a Google API, such as:
    - *"Built with the Google Gemini™ API. Google Gemini™, Google Chrome™, and Gmail™ are trademarks of Google LLC. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google LLC."*
    - *"Connects to Google Gemini™ (a product of Google LLC) through your own Google Account."*
  - Avoid unstructured taglines or prominent headers that read as if Google co-created or sponsors the game (`https://developers.google.com/terms` Section 6b, read 2026-11-10).
  - Always use `Gemini™`, `Google Chrome™`, and `Gmail™` as adjectives modifying a generic noun (for example, *"the Google Gemini™ assistant"* or *"the Google Gemini™ API"*), never as a possessive, plural, or verb, and distinguish the mark with the `™` symbol on first or prominent appearance (`https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/trademark/` and `https://about.google/brand-resource-center/trademark-list/`, read 2026-11-10).
- **Logo and button rules (`https://about.google/brand-resource-center/rules/` and `https://developers.google.com/identity/gsi/web/guides/branding`, read 2026-11-10):**
  - **No standalone Google or Gemini logos in the game UI:** Under `https://about.google/brand-resource-center/rules/` (read 2026-11-10), third parties may not place the Google "G" logo or the Gemini spark icon inside third-party product interfaces or icons without explicit written permission or a qualifying partner badge agreement, and may never animate, recolor, or alter a Google logo.
  - **Prominence:** DREAM Online's own title and interface branding must always be substantially larger and more prominent than any plain-text mention of Google Gemini™ (`https://about.google/brand-resource-center/rules/`, read 2026-11-10).
  - **Sign in with Google button:** If the HUD renders a Google sign-in button, it must follow the **Sign in with Google Branding Guidelines** (`https://developers.google.com/identity/gsi/web/guides/branding`, read 2026-11-10): render it via the official Google Identity Services SDK or approved configurator, keep the standard four-color Google "G" on its approved white tile inside the standard light, dark, or neutral button container, never use a monochrome "G" or standalone icon without button text, and use only approved button text (`"Sign in with Google"`, `"Sign up with Google"`, or `"Continue with Google"`).

## 6. Free-Tier Data: What must players be told before they use the HUD?

**Verdict:** **Before any player uses a HUD feature backed by the Unpaid (Free Tier) Gemini API, we must show a clear in-app disclosure and privacy policy notice stating four mandatory facts: (1) Google uses free-tier prompts, inputs, and generated responses to provide, improve, and develop Google products, services, and machine learning technologies; (2) human reviewers may read, annotate, and process those prompts and responses (after Google disconnects them from the Google Account, API key, and Cloud project); (3) players must never submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information; and (4) the assistant is Google LLC's product governed by the Google Privacy Policy, and our app's use of Google API data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use.**

- **What Google's Gemini API Additional Terms say on Unpaid Services:** Under **Unpaid Services — How Google Uses Your Data** in the **Gemini API Additional Terms of Service** (`https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms` and `https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms`, effective March 23, 2026, read 2026-11-10):
  - *"When you use Unpaid Services, including, for example, Google AI Studio and the unpaid quota on Gemini API, Google uses the content you submit to the Services and any generated responses to provide, improve, and develop Google products and services and machine learning technologies, including Google's enterprise features, products, and services, consistent with our Privacy Policy."*
  - *"To help with quality and improve our products, human reviewers may read, annotate, and process your API input and output. Google takes steps to protect your privacy as part of this process. This includes disconnecting this data from your Google Account, API key, and Cloud project before reviewers see or annotate it. **Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to the Unpaid Services.**"*
- **What Google's API Terms and User Data Policy require us to tell players:** Under **Section 3d (User Privacy and API Clients)** of the **Google APIs Terms of Service** (`https://developers.google.com/terms`, read 2026-11-10) and the **Google API Services User Data Policy** (`https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy`, read 2026-11-10), our privacy policy and pre-consent HUD screen must prominently disclose what data is collected, how it is used, and how it is shared, and must include the mandatory Limited Use statement:
  - *"DREAM Online's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements."*
- **Recommended pre-use consent text to show 18+ players before activating any free-tier Gemini API HUD feature:**
  > *"18+ Notice: This optional feature connects to Google Gemini™, a product of Google LLC, not DREAM Online. On the unpaid free tier, Google LLC uses the prompts and responses you submit to provide, improve, and develop Google products, services, and machine learning models, and human reviewers may read and annotate them after they are disconnected from your account (see the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy). Do not speak or type sensitive, confidential, or personal information (including private emails, passwords, or real-world personal details). You must be 18 or older and located outside the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK to use the unpaid Gemini API tier."*
