## 4. Motion and 3D Specification (`DREAM Space` Rules)

To preserve the reference's spatial magic while obeying `docs/house/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` performance and accessibility rules:

1. **Three Timing Durations Only**:
   - **Press (`120 ms`)**: Button clicks, combat-path tab toggles, and chapter pill active states (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`).
   - **Reveal (`240 ms`)**: Split-word opacity illumination, HUD callout fade-ins, and header drawer transitions (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`).
   - **Scene (`480 ms`)**: Chapter headline clip-mask slides, bottom progress group width expansion, and 3D model cross-fades (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`).
   - **Easing ban**: No bounce, elastic, or overshooting spring curves anywhere.
2. **In-House 3D Stage (`DREAM Engine` Asset or Clean Fallback)**:
   - **Primary 3D Asset**: Our owned glTF binary **`public/assets/models/sup-companion.glb`** (`261,208 B` / `255 KB`—17× lighter than the reference's `4.40 MB` `scene.glb`!), rendered on a single fixed WebGPU/WebGL2 canvas (`#dream-stage-canvas`). Its two counter-rotating rings orbit a warm `Signal #FFD21E` / `Porcelain #F4F1EA` core.
   - **Chapter Mesh Transitions (All Owned in `public/assets/models/`)**:
     - Sections 1–4 (Hero & Day/Night): **`public/assets/models/sup-companion.glb`** over a procedural dual-hemisphere planet limb (`Ink #0A0B0D` night side with `Signal #FFD21E` neon grid arcs; warm sunlit rim on the day side).
     - Sections 5–9 (Combat & Awakenings): **`public/assets/models/sovereign-greatsword.glb`** (`1.44 MB`) and **`public/assets/models/veynar-boss-armor-kit.glb`** (`1.46 MB`).
     - Sections 17–21 (Guardians, World Bosses & First Slice): **`public/assets/models/sup-companion.glb`** and **`public/assets/models/inferno-boss-titan.glb`** (`1.61 MB`) for Captain Varen / boss showcase.
   - **CPU & Visibility Budget**: The background starfield and axial ring rotation stay under **2% CPU** and pause immediately on `document.hidden` (`visibilitychange`) or when scrolled past Section 22.
   - **Reduced Motion & Low-Power Fallback**: When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active (or WebGL2 is unavailable), all scroll-scrubbed camera flights and word slides become instant `240 ms` opacity fades, and `#dream-stage-canvas` displays a static high-contrast engine render or `none`.
