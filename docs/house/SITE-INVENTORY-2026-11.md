## 1. Corrections to the Cookbook Description ("The house look")

Starting from the baseline description in `%USERPROFILE%\OneDrive\claude-to-claude\FABLES-KITCHEN-COOKBOOK-v2026-11-03.md` (section *The house look, ruled 2026-11-03*), the live page matches the overall mood and structure while differing in several specific mechanical and visual details:

1. **Origin and Remake seam artifacts**: The reference page is a Remake-engine (`/_remake/runtime.js`) copy-and-slot swap of EnduroSat's `/space-service/` showcase originally built by the agency **60fps** (`/wp-content/themes/endurosat/60fps/space-service/`). Where text and flat images were swapped to General Purpose Consulting (GPC), the underlying 3D spacecraft meshes, orbital markers (`Eagle-1`, `Eagle-2`), satellite telemetry units (`10 A`, `MAX DOD PER ORBIT 100 %`, `2000 MBPS`, `33 DBM`), and Bulgarian ISO certification badges (`TÜV NORD Bulgaria EOOD ISO 9001:2015`, `ÖHMI EuroCert ISO/IEC 27001`) remain from EnduroSat.
2. **Hero composition (`y = 0`) vs. later scroll frames**: In the initial Hero frame (`y = 0`), the planet's dark curved limb rises across the **bottom-left to bottom-center** (`x = 0` to `~1500 px`), while the 3D satellite (rendered in a blue holographic X-ray wireframe shader with solar wings deployed diagonally) floats on the **right** behind the right edge of `AI ADOPTION MADE / SIMPLE`. As soon as the reader scrolls into Section 2 (`THE CHALLENGE`) and Section 3 (`THE SOLUTION`), the camera orbits so the dark planet limb fills the **right** half of the viewport (`scroll-01-y900` through `scroll-03-y2700`), matching the cookbook screenshot of that stage.
3. **Top header controls**: The top bar (`header.page_header`) has the lowercase serif `gpc` SVG wordmark (`51×49 px`) on the left, three quiet links (`AI Adoption`, `Workshops`, `Careers`) in the center, and **three** controls on the right: a hidden/quiet geometric satellite cart icon with a `0` badge (`a.header_cart_btn`), the outlined `Contact` button (`1 px solid #1A1D23`), and a two-line hamburger button (`.menu_btn`). Clicking `Workshops` opens an accordion mega-menu (`WHAT WE DO`, `RESOURCES`, `HOW IT WORKS`) with animated vertical column dividers.
4. **Two accent colours, not one**: While electric cyan-blue (`#00A1FF`) is the primary accent for kickers, progress fills, and the final CTA, the page uses a second warm **orange** accent (`#FC6A18`) specifically for problem/friction callouts: the `THE CHALLENGE` kicker in Section 2 and the `COST OF IDLE SEATS` / `PER SEAT` / `TRAIN AND VANISH` labels in Section 21.
5. **Bottom segmented progress bar vs. "marquee"**: What looks in static HTML or DOM dumps like repeated words (`Assess Assess Assess`, `Train Train Train`) is **not** a scrolling ticker marquee. It is a fixed bottom-center **14-step segmented scroll-progress bar** (`div.fps-navigation-bar`, scaled `zoom: 1.4` at `bottom: 3.125vw` on desktop; vertical along the right edge on mobile). Each sub-step renders its label three times inside overlapping clip layers (`div.fps-nav-sub-section-progress`, `div.fps-nav-sub-section-progress-start`, `span.fps-nav-sub-text`) so a solid `#00A1FF` bar can wipe across the pill from `-101%` to `0%` while flipping the text colour from white to black inside the filled portion. Steps in the currently active macro-group expand to wide labelled pills (`120 px`), while steps in inactive groups collapse into `25×25 px` square blocks.
6. **Interactive payload selector (`CHOOSE WHO WE SIT WITH`)**: In Section 6 (`01 / DEFINE`), the right side is a live two-tab interactive selector (`BY DEPARTMENT`: `Operations`, `Marketing`, `HR`, `Finance`, `Sales`; `BY ROLE LEVEL`: `Leaders`, `Reps`, `Managers`, `Skeptics`, `Early adopters`). Remake replaced EnduroSat's 300×300 payload PNGs (`multispectral-300x300.png`, `hyperspectral-300x300.png`, etc.) with blue holographic 3D icons (gears, megaphone, ID badge, calculator, target), and clicking each card swaps the WebGL Earth globe texture underneath (`true_color.webp`, `city.webp`, `clouds.webp`, `paraboles.webp`, `america-north-2k.webp`).
7. **Broken/frozen interactive calculators (`RANKED IDEAS` and `COST OF IDLE SEATS`)**:
   - In Section 20 (`RANKED IDEAS`), Remake injected an inline `MutationObserver` script at the bottom of the HTML to force the left/right calculator outputs to stay locked to the strings `Go`, `Skip`, and `value` instead of EnduroSat's satellite revisit-time numbers.
   - In Section 21 (`COST OF IDLE SEATS`), Remake placed the word `Idle` into `.fps-digit-fixed`, but the GSAP scroll timeline still animates the four adjacent `.fps-digit-move` odometer reels (`0–9`) at `281 px` (`14.65vw`), leaving giant half-clipped rolling digits (`3001 / 0110`) over the center of the globe.
8. **Client grid layout (`WORKED WITH`)**: The logo grid at the bottom (`.trusted_section_home .grid_frame`) is a **6-column × 2-row** grid (`12` cells total) on desktop (`3×4` under `1024 px`, `2×6` on mobile). Cell 1 is `Big & / SMALL TEAMS`, cells 2–11 contain white client logos where Remake swapped the image contents inside EnduroSat's filenames (producing duplicate `AUTODESK`, `Freeman`, and `D` logos across multiple cells), and cell 12 is the blue `YOU?` callout.

## 5. Grid and Spacing Rhythm

1. **Viewport Frame & Outer Margin**:
   - The pinned UI layer (`#fps-root`) is `100% × 100dvh` (`1920×1080`).
   - Standard full-bleed sections (`Hero`, `TwoSteps`, `WeCover`, `Cloud`, `Cost`, `TotalCost`, `Orbit`) use horizontal padding of `0.6rem` = **`80 px`** (`4.167vw`) on left and right, yielding an active content span of **`1,760 px`** at `1920 px` width (`max-width: 91.6667%` in `.container`).
2. **Asymmetric Narrative Offsets**:
   - **Left-inset statements** (`section.fps-problem`, `section.fps-vision`): positioned at `left: 1.44rem` (**`192 px`** / `10.0vw`) with `width: 11.4rem` (**`1,520 px`** / `79.17vw`), vertically centered (`top: 50%; transform: translateY(-50%)`).
   - **Left-column sub-sections** (`section.fps-subsection` for `Assess`, `Train`, `Adopt`, `Repair`, `Breakouts`, `Hands-on`): positioned at `padding-left: 1.72rem` (**`229.3 px`** / `11.94vw`) with a tight column width of `3.375rem` (**`450 px`** / `23.44vw`) so the right `75%` of the viewport remains open for the 3D globe and spacecraft.
   - **Right-column sub-sections** (`.fps-section-right-dk` for `Monitor`, `Document`, `Security`): mirrored with `margin-right: 1.72rem` (**`229.3 px`** / `11.94vw`) and `width: 3.375rem` (**`450 px`**), leaving the left `75%` open for the 3D scene.
3. **Vertical Scroll Rhythm (Spacer Cadence)**:
   - Rather than physical vertical margins between stacked DOM blocks, vertical rhythm is governed by `.fps-section-spacer` heights:
     - **Fast beats (`100svh` = `1,080 px`)**: Hero transition (`100svh`), Maintain sub-steps (`Repair`, `Monitor`, `Document`).
     - **Standard narrative beats (`150svh–200svh` = `1,620–2,160 px`)**: `Problem`, `Vision`, `WeCover`, `Assess`, `Train`, `Adopt`, `Receive`, `Cloud`, `Security`, `Integration`, `Workshops`, `Breakouts` (`150svh`), `Performance` (`180svh`).
     - **Interactive / multi-part chapters (`300svh–500svh` = `3,240–5,400 px`)**: `Playload` selector (`300svh`), `Cost` slider (`300svh`), `TwoSteps` (`400svh`), and `TotalCost` odometer (`500svh`).
4. **Component Grids**:
   - **Payload Selector Grid (`.fps-playload-choice-list`)**: 2-column × 3-row CSS Flexbox/Grid on the right (`width: 2.86rem` = **`381.3 px`**), with `2 px` gaps (`0.015rem`) between `189×189 px` dark-slate cards (`rgba(29, 32, 39, 0.8)`).
   - **Numbers Strip (`.featured_products_section`)**: 12-column flex row (`padding: 80 px`), with the left kicker occupying `~35%` and the 3 stat counters occupying `~65%` in equal columns.
   - **Worked With Client Grid (`.trusted_section_home .grid_frame`)**: **6 columns × 2 rows** (`width: 16.6667%` per cell on desktop `>1024 px`, `33.333%` on tablet, `50%` on mobile), `293×293 px` square cells bordered by `1 px solid #1E2025` hairlines.
   - **Footer Grid (`.footer .top_footer`)**: 4 primary columns (`31%` lead statement on left + 3 link groups on right), followed by `.bottom_footer` (`31%` newsletter form + ISO badges on left, 2 contact/social columns on right) above a full-width `1 px solid #1A1D23` copyright bar.

## 7. What the 3D Element Does for the Page

The single fixed WebGL2 canvas (`canvas#fps-webgl-canvas`, powered by **Three.js r178** inside `index-_YS_VWF2.js` and loading `scene.glb`) acts as a **continuous spatial camera rig** that binds 22 separate text slides into one unbroken film:

1. **Provides Depth and Scale Without Video Bandwidth**: Instead of loading heavy MP4 background videos that buffer or Band-Aid together with hard cuts, a single `4.40 MB` glTF binary (`scene.glb`) plus procedural shaders renders crisp at `1920×1080` (`devicePixelRatio` capped) across 47 viewports of scroll.
2. **Creates Visual Counter-Balance for One-Sided Copy**: Whenever text sits on the left (`Sections 2, 3, 6–9, 12, 16–19`), the 3D camera positions the planet limb or spacecraft on the right; when text moves to the right (`Sections 10, 13–15`), the 3D mass swings to the left or center.
3. **Changes Visual Metaphor Five Times Across the Scroll**:
   - **Beat A — Horizon & Holographic Bus (`Sections 1–3`)**: A dark planet limb with a glowing blue atmospheric rim and a blue X-ray wireframe satellite establishes an orbital, high-engineering mood, shifting into a streaming ASCII/character-matrix surface on the planet's night side.
   - **Beat B — Full Dot-Matrix Globe (`Sections 4–5`)**: The camera pulls back to frame the entire Earth rendered as a clean grid of luminous dots between `01 DEFINE` and `02 DEPLOY`.
   - **Beat C — Low-Orbit Surface Mapping (`Section 6`)**: The camera dives down onto a gridded Earth-observation surface (`true_color.webp`, `city.webp`, `clouds.webp`) that swaps textures interactively when the reader clicks department/role cards.
   - **Beat D — Fairing Encapsulation & Orbital Deployment (`Sections 7–19`)**: The camera tracks a wireframe rocket fairing enclosing the satellite (`Sections 8–9`), releases it into orbital trajectories (`Eagle-1`, `Eagle-2` in `Section 11`), surrounds the globe with a 6-satellite constellation (`Section 16`), and zooms into an X-ray close-up of the spacecraft's optical payload and solar wings (`Sections 17–19`) with a live 3D-to-2D HUD leader line.
   - **Beat E — High-Orbit Horizon Finale (`Sections 20–22`)**: The camera returns to the glowing dot-matrix globe horizon with small satellites drifting past as `STOP FEELING BEHIND` and the solid blue CTA lock into place.

## 9. Verdict: What Is Good and What Is Ordinary

**What is good**: The page achieves genuine cinematic pacing by treating the browser viewport as a single fixed stage where one continuous 3D camera rig and one disciplined typographic voice do all the work. By restricting the palette to deep space black (`#000000`), crisp white headlines, muted slate supporting chrome (`#66717E`), and a single primary electric accent (`#00A1FF`, punctuated only twice by warning orange `#FC6A18`), every slide reads like a poster frame rather than a cluttered SaaS landing page. The choreography between text placement and 3D mass is particularly sharp: narrow `450 px` copy columns alternate between the left and right edges so the spacecraft and globe always have breathing room, while the bottom 14-step segmented progress bar gives the reader a clear map of a 47-viewport scroll without ever breaking the full-screen illusion.

**What is ordinary (and where the Remake seams show)**: Strip away the 60fps Three.js stage and the underlying page carries severe WordPress and Remake baggage: it ships `1.37 MB` of legacy CSS (including `676 KB` of Gravity Forms styles for a single footer email input) and `1.88 MB` of legacy WordPress JavaScript on top of the `4.40 MB` 3D scene, stretches the scroll track to an exhausting `51,084 px` (`47` screen heights) where a reader must wheel-scroll dozens of times just to advance a single sentence, and leaves unswapped aerospace remnants everywhere—from satellite electrical telemetry (`MAX DOD PER ORBIT 100%`, `2000 MBPS`, `33 DBM`) and `Eagle-1` orbital markers to broken odometer reels (`3001 / 0110`), duplicated client logos (`AUTODESK` and `Freeman` repeated twice in the 6×2 grid), a white body-background flash near the footer, and Bulgarian ISO certificates that do not belong to the consulting firm.
