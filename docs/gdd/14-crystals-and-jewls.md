## 2. Forge Crystals at Fable's

### Core Purpose & Economy
- Crystals provide an enhancement layer on top of weapons and armor at Fable's (town anvil, truck, and summoned truck, with one shared price list).
- Crystals are not the gear itself and not the base gear forge.
- **Stat & Rarity Law:** Crystals never add attack or defense and never change rarity. They add visual effects and recovery-type stats only.
- **Salvaging Loop:** Pulverizing (salvaging) items pulls gear out of circulation so the Marketplace does not flood, keeping items rare. Crystals and materials can be traded on the Marketplace for Sols. Crystals are never sold for cash, and cash can never buy in-game currency.
- Enhancing never breaks, downgrades, or destroys equipment.

### Salvage Yields
1. **Common or Uncommon item:** 2 tier 1 crystals.
2. **Rare item:** 1 tier 2 crystal and 2 tier 1 crystals.
3. **Forged tier 3 item:** 1 tier 3 crystal, 2 tier 2 crystals, and 4 tier 1 crystals.
   - A tier 3 item is forged from two copies of the same Rare drop (e.g. a melee copy and an HP copy). There is no lottery: nothing is lost, and no stats are lost.
   - Salvaging 30 Rare drops (15 forged pairs) yields the 15 tier 3 crystals required to max out a piece, while simultaneously covering all required tier 2 and tier 1 crystals. Grinding is the sole progression path.

### Enhancement Stages & Crystal Costs
- **Level Gating:** 1 stage every 4 character levels (levels 4, 8, 12, ... up to 60) for a total of 15 stages. Each weapon and armor piece upgrades along its own track. Levels 61 and 62 keep their canon reward of +10 to all stats and add no stage.
- **Costs per piece (plus Sols fee):**
  - **Stages 1–5 (Levels 4–20):** 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 tier 1 crystals (30 tier 1 total).
  - **Stages 6–10 (Levels 24–40):** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 tier 2 crystals (15 tier 2 total).
  - **Stages 11–15 (Levels 44–60):** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 tier 3 crystals (15 tier 3 total).

### Stat per Stage (Player's Choice)
Each stage, the player chooses one stat bonus:
- **Cooldown reduction:** +0.5% per pick, capped at 20% total from crystal upgrades.
- **Mana recovery:** +1% per pick, capped at 40%.
- **Stamina:** +1% per pick, capped at 40%.
- **Energy:** +1% per pick, capped at 40%.
- The forge screen displays the caps before the player spends any crystals or Sols.

### Visual Effects
- **Weapons:** Visual effect density builds with each stage from a faint trace at stage 1 to full density at stage 15. At Fable's, players can choose between glow, flame, or smoke, and can swap between them for free.
- **Armor Wings:** Wings sprout on every armor piece, growing from a small nub at stage 1 to full wings at stage 15. The underlying armor model never changes.
  - Stages 1–5: Rough dark iron.
  - Stages 6–10: Polished steel and silver.
  - Stages 11–14: Platinum.
  - Stage 15: Platinum with diamond inlay.
- **Open Question:** Whether the crystal effect shows on top of a rarity's aura or replaces it.

## 3. Jewls, Accessories, and Sockets

### NPC & Shop
- **Jewls:** The jewelry vendor and accessory enhancer. Jewls represents Google and Gemini's NPC (a nod to Jules, Google's coding agent, and sounding like jewels, without using brand spelling).
- **Shop:** Jewls' Jewelry Box.

### Accessory Slots & Sockets
- **Six Accessory Slots:** 1 necklace (the strongest piece and build centerpiece), 2 rings, 2 earrings, and 1 bracelet.
- **Sockets (10 Sockets Total Fully Enhanced):**
  - Necklace: 3 sockets.
  - Rings: 2 sockets each (4 total).
  - Earrings: 1 socket each (2 total).
  - Bracelet: 2 sockets.
- **Socket Enhancement:** Enhancing an accessory at Jewls unlocks sockets one at a time. It costs crystals and Sols, with zero failure, zero downgrades, and zero risk of item destruction.

### Gemstones, Elements, and Tiers
Each gemstone provides an equal amount of elemental damage and elemental resistance:
1. **Ruby (Red):** Fire.
2. **Sapphire (Blue):** Water and Ice.
3. **Emerald (Green):** Earth and Nature.
4. **Topaz (Yellow):** Lightning.
5. **Amethyst (Purple):** Dream and Shadow.
6. **Diamond (White):** Light.

#### Guaranteed Gem Tiers (No Lottery)
- **Tier 1 (Chip):** +1% elemental damage and resistance. Purchased from Jewls for Sols.
- **Tier 2 (Cut):** +2% elemental damage and resistance. Crafted by combining 3 Chips of the same gem.
- **Tier 3 (Flawless):** +4% elemental damage and resistance. Forged by combining 3 Cut gems of the same gem.
- **Tier 4 (Fossilized):** +6% elemental damage and resistance. Created by crushing 1 Opal together with 2 Flawless gems of the same gem.
- **Opals:** Rare drops exclusively from monsters and bosses (drop rates set by mob design); fully tradeable on the Marketplace. Jewls never sells Opals.
- **Unsocketing:** Gems can be removed from sockets for free at any time without breaking, enabling players to freely rebuild their setups.

### Stat Caps & Monetization Boundary
- **Element Cap:** Each element is capped at +30% damage and +30% resistance from gems. The jeweler's interface displays the cap before any purchase or craft.
- **Monetization Law:** Gems, Opals, crystals, and accessories are strictly earned or bought with in-game Sols, and traded only on the player Marketplace. Cash never buys any of them, and cash never buys Sols.
