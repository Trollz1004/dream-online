# Wardrobe Catalogue — Wave One

Status: ruled by Josh 2026-11-05 (NEEDs Shop v1.1 design sheet), revised 2026-11-07 for the stat rule (`docs/gdd/GAME-SYSTEMS-RULING-2026-11-07.md`)
Audience: Codex, Claude, Gemini, game designers, UI engineers

Wave one items for the DREAM Online wardrobe. The wardrobe is the NEEDs shop (`docs/gdd/12-wardrobe-needs-shop.md`).
The character creator shapes a body only and everyone leaves it in kid-safe basics (`docs/gdd/07-character-creation.md`).
Items carry stats under the stat rule below: the same percentage per tier, the choice is which stat, everything cash-sold is also earnable in game, and cash buys time, not power.
Payment accounts follow `docs/house/PAYMENT-ACCOUNTS-RULE.md`: money for this game goes through the game's own Square account and nothing else.
Prices are `[YOUR PRICE]` placeholders until Josh says finalize. Percentages Josh has not ruled are `[TIER %]` and stats not ruled are `[STAT]`.

## Core Rules

1. **Bought is owned.** A skin a player acquires stays theirs forever. No rental, no season lock, no expiration.
2. **Currency Store Release (3 months at most).** Items sold through the game's own Square account reach the in-game coin store (Sols / in-game currency) after 3 months at most (February 2027 for Wave One).
3. **The game is free to play. This shop sells looks, time and choice at the same percentages everyone can earn; nothing here buys power.**
4. **The Stat Rule.** Items carry stats. Every item in a tier gives the same percentage; the player's choice is which stat, never how much. Everything sold for cash can also be earned or bought in game, at an in-game price high enough that the cash version is a convenience of time, never an advantage of power. Every skin fits any body.
5. **The Founder Pet Exception.** `TrollinlootNScoot` is the one skin that never reaches the coin store (the founder pet stays never). It is cosmetic only.
6. **Blu's Tee Currency Rule.** Blu's Tee is bought with Blue Charge only; it is never sold in the coin store or through the game's own Square account. It is cosmetic only.
7. **Kid-Safe Basics.** Always owned by every character, never sold.

## Tiers and percentages

A tier is a group of items that give the same percentage. Within a tier the player picks which stat; the number never changes.

## Detailed Notes by Item Group

### 1. AFK Fishing Set in zebra fish scale
- **Pieces (4):** Hat (`afk-fishing-hat`), Vest (`afk-fishing-top`), Waders (`afk-fishing-bottom`), Boots (`afk-fishing-shoes`).
- **How Earned:** The game's own Square account (NEEDs shop purchase).
- **Coin Store:** Reaches in-game coin store in February 2027 (3 months at most from release), so every piece is earnable in game.
- **Stat:** 10 percent fishing experience. The hat is a hat, and any hat is 10 percent; the set's other pieces carry the same 10 percent.

### 2. Cooking Apron and Chef's Hat
- **Pieces (2):** Cooking Apron (`cooking-apron`, top slot) and Chef's Hat (`chefs-hat`, headwear). Both fit any body.
- **How Earned:** The game's own Square account (NEEDs shop purchase).
- **Coin Store:** Reaches in-game coin store in February 2027 (3 months at most from release).
- **Stat:** 10 percent cooking experience each.

### 3. Team Blue Baseball Uniform
- **Pieces (4):** Cap (`team-blue-baseball-cap`), Jersey (`team-blue-baseball-jersey`), Pants (`team-blue-baseball-pants`), Cleats (`team-blue-baseball-cleats`).
- **How Earned:** The game's own Square account (NEEDs shop purchase).
- **Coin Store:** Reaches in-game coin store in February 2027 (3 months at most from release).
- **Customization:** Player chooses a two-digit jersey number (00 to 99) at time of purchase.
- **Stat:** The cap is a hat: 10 percent experience. Jersey, pants and cleats carry the same percentage as their tier's other choices: `[TIER %]`, stat `[STAT]`, both for Josh to rule.

### 4. Team Blue Blunt Weapon Skins
- **Pieces (3):** Team Blue Ash Bat (`team-blue-ash-bat`), Team Blue Alloy Bat (`team-blue-alloy-bat`), Plastic T-Ball Bat (`plastic-t-ball-bat`).
- **How Earned:** The game's own Square account (NEEDs shop purchase).
- **Coin Store:** Reaches in-game coin store in February 2027 (3 months at most from release).
- **Rule:** Compatible as overlays for any one-handed blunt weapon (hammer, club, mace). Each bat carries the same percentage as the other Team Blue choices, `[TIER %]`; the choice between them is the look and the stat, never the amount.

### 5. Blu's Tee
- **Piece (1):** Blu's Tee (`blus-pet-tee`).
- **Visual:** Oversized white pet tee printed "You're my boy blue!!!".
- **How Earned:** Bought with Blue Charge only. Blue Charge is the one-off currency shed as sparks during encounters with Blu, the boss of bosses (`docs/gdd/canon-lore-inhouse.md`).
- **Rule:** Cosmetic only; it carries no stat. Never in the coin store; never through the game's own Square account. Cannot be purchased with coins or real-world money.

### 6. TrollinlootNScoot (Founder Pet)
- **Piece (1):** TrollinlootNScoot (`TrollinlootNScoot`).
- **Visual & AI:** Troll riding an original drift cart, T1 Live NPC memory, clean dad jokes.
- **How Earned:** The game's own Square account (founder-funding limited edition in NEEDs shop).
- **Rule:** Cosmetic only; it carries no stat. Never in the coin store. The sole permanent exception to the 3 months at most currency store rule, and so the one cash-sold item with no in-game path. The founder pet stays never.

### 7. Kid-Safe Basics
- **Pieces (3):** Plain Tee (`basics-top`), Plain Trousers (`basics-bottom`), Plain Sneakers (`basics-shoes`).
- **Rule:** Always owned by every character upon exit from character creation. Free, cannot be deleted or unequipped without replacement, never sold in the shop or coin store. They are the plain default look and carry no bonus.
