# DREAM ONLINE Vision

## Pitch

DREAM ONLINE is a live-world open-world MMO with no-tab-target action combat, deep life-skill systems, one shared world server direction, no fast travel, and live NPCs that remember, react, and evolve.

Dream logic allows impossible-feeling world events as long as the player experience
stays readable and technically honest. A wilderness trail can become city life during
a live Dream event, a quiet road can wake into a festival, and a recovery rider like
C0D3X can cross the sky on a dragon-scale companion when the world needs visible
repair. These moments should be built as controlled event layers, streaming masks,
lighting/audio shifts, NPC behavior swaps, and player-visible rituals before promising
full seamless world replacement.

## Pillars

1. Real action combat, not tab-target rotation.
2. Life skills are main progression, not side chores.
3. The world changes on a real schedule.
4. PvP is dangerous but rule-bound.
5. Live NPCs create memory, consequence, and spectacle.
6. Convenience can be sold; power cannot.

## Core Fantasy

The player is not only a fighter. The player is a worker, fisher, cook, hunter, trader, farmer, crafter, guildmate, explorer, and night-city risk taker.

## Canon To Preserve

- Sup@ is the companion sphere and primary game voice.
- THE BAN HAMMER turns anti-cheat into visible server lore.
- C0D3X is the rollback rider and represents verified world-state recovery.
- NEEDs are in-game currency/product only.
- Monetization remains pay-for-convenience and cosmetics, never pay-to-win.

## Secrecy Boundary

Founder-only end-game material stays in the private OneDrive do-not-commit vault. Public docs and repo docs may only point to the internal design vault at a high level. Do not reproduce the reveal, quotes, encounter mechanics, or hidden plot material here.

## First Fun Hypothesis

If combat feels good in a small field, and the same field also supports gathering, fishing, crafting, durability repair, PvP flagging, and a night-state event, the MMO foundation is real.
