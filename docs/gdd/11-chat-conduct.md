# Chat Conduct: Sup@ Keeps the Vibe

Founder ruling, 2026-09-28, in his shape. The rule of the game's chat is positive energy, and Sup@ is the one AI allowed to let players' moods reach it, because keeping the vibe is its job.

## The ladder

1. **Stop clowning.** When chat turns to rage, over anything, Sup@ steps in with one line in the clown's own register: stop clowning. It changes the vibe and nothing else. No name, no score, no lecture.
2. **I can be Sup@.** If the clowning goes on, Sup@ says the second line: "or I can be Sup@," with the clown faces, and for a set time everything that player tries to say in all-chat comes out as one fixed line instead: "I love Trollz, Trollz loves me, we only say Sup@ troll things, so troll along with me." The player can still play, still trade, still talk to their party. Only all-chat is swapped, and only for the set time.
3. **The chat ban.** Past that, a chat ban, and each one is longer than the last. The lengths are tuning and stay in the private tuning sheet.

## Who enforces

The ladder runs on the deterministic moderation pipeline the README's governance lock describes: evidence, rule check, then the step. Sup@ is the voice and the face of every step, never the judge; the troll in the cart can heckle the clown ("one day Sup@ will join us too, and sing along") and Sup@ answers with its two words. The founder's authority over the rule is authority over what the rule is; it is not a ban button, per the Drift Cart doctrine in `README.md`. GeminEYE feeds the pipeline what it sees in chat (`docs/gdd/09-interface-style.md`).

## What the ladder never does

- Never names, guesses or records anything about who is playing nearby, their age or their household. The younger-players lane (`docs/gdd/00-vision.md`) has its own always-clean content; the conduct ladder is the same for everyone and knows nothing about anyone.
- Never mocks the player, per the pet and tutorial rulings. The clown line is a bit, not a judgement.
- Never leaves the game: no line refers to real money, accounts outside the game, or anything the compliance wall keeps out.
