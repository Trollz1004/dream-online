# Equipment and Costume Slots

Status: canon ruling, recorded 2026-11-12 from Joshua's rulings (01:25 and 02:59) by the Claude judge lane
Audience: UI design, client engineering, game systems, art direction

## 1. Equipment Slots

1. **Weapons:** Main hand, off hand, and awakening weapon.
2. **Armor pieces:** Standard combat armor slots.
3. **Jewelry:** Necklace, rings, earrings, and bracelet, equipped with gem slots (see Jewls' gems and accessories in `docs/gdd/14-crystals-and-jewls.md`).

## 2. Costume Slots (Appearance Layer)

1. Costume slots sit over the gear as the visual look of the character.
2. The number of costume slots varies by costume.
3. **Expensive costumes:** An expensive costume fills more slots; it includes skins for the main weapon, the off hand, and the awakening weapon.
4. **Life-skill costumes:** A life-skill costume is only a chest skin and a helm skin.
5. **Mix and match:** Players may freely mix parts from any costumes—combat or life skill—to look better or to be funny. (Founder's example: a fishing costume's fish head worn with a swimsuit bottom in PvP).
6. **Costume set effects:** Costumes carry a set effect as a stat.
7. **Whole-set reading (pending Josh):** Claude's reading, pending Josh: the set effect needs the whole set worn, so mixing parts for looks is a free choice that gives up that set's effect.

## 3. Life-Skill Outfit Bonuses (Confirmed)

1. The shop outfit's wear cut applies to every tool, the cheap NPC throwaway tools included.
2. The outfit also increases skill speed, alongside the helm's skill EXP. Speed is the main reason to wear it.
