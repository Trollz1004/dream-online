# PvP Flagging, Durability, and Repair

## PvP Philosophy

PvP should create danger and stories, not drive normal players out of the world.

## Zone Types

| Zone | PvP Rule |
|---|---|
| Sanctuary | No PvP, no hostile combat, town/social/crafting safe zone |
| Frontier | Opt-in PvP flagging, bonus rewards when flagged |
| Rival Road | Flagged players can fight; unflagged protected unless they attack |
| Blackout Zone | Night-only high-risk area with better rewards |
| Guild War Zone | Guild/faction objectives with explicit participation |

## Flag States

- Peaceful.
- Flagged.
- Aggressor.
- Outlaw.
- Bounty Target.
- Guild War Participant.

## Consequence Rules

- Attacking flagged players is clean.
- Attacking peaceful players in allowed danger contexts creates aggressor/outlaw state.
- Repeated unlawful kills create bounty visibility and town service restrictions.
- Outlaws can repair only at black-market/camp systems at higher cost.

## Durability

Combat gear durability loss:

- Small loss on normal combat use.
- More loss on death.
- More loss on outlaw death.
- Gear is never lost to an upgrade: no downgrade, no breaking, no disappearing, jewels and accessories included. No upgrade ever downgrades, destroys, or makes disappear any weapon, armor, jewel, or accessory (a red-name death drop is a PvP punishment, not an upgrade).

Life tool durability loss:

- Per gather action.
- Per fishing cast.
- Per processing/crafting use for stations.

Repair sources:

- Town blacksmith.
- Player-crafted repair kits.
- Portable anvil.
- Camp repair station.
- Guild workshop later.

## Portable Anvil Rule

Portable anvil is convenience, not free power. It repairs in the field but consumes repair materials/currency and has cooldown or limited charges.

## Founder ruling 2026-11-10: red names and Muse Guards

### Red-Name (Outlaw) Rules

Red name is the outlaw state:

- Killing 3 players outside township battles turns a player red.
- **Area-attack multi-kill turns red at once at any level:** Level has nothing to do with it; the rule is the same for a new player and a veteran party. A player whose area attack lands the killing hit on more than one player turns red at once, with no 3-kill count (the point: a bully learns to use single-target skills and kill one player at a time). Higher levels still have their normal advantage in a fight, but that is not what this rule is about.
- **Area attacks pull mob aggro:** Area attacks also hit and anger nearby mobs. Someone who drops an area attack on a group fighting hard-hitting mobs pulls those mobs onto himself, and they can kill him.
- **Death while red drops gear:** If he dies while red, to players or to the mobs he angered, he drops gear by the red-name rule.
- Red status lasts 1 hour for each player kill.
- A red-named player who is killed drops half of their gear.

### Muse Guards

- Town guards are Muse Guards, with a little Muse AI in the mix.
- Muse Guards one-shot red-named players. Melee guards close to melee range instantly with a speed-burst dash, and patrol guards kill a red player with one hit.
